# Code of Conduct

## Standards

- Be respectful. Disagree without hostility.
- No harassment, abuse, or discrimination based on identity.
- Stay on topic: Scriptoribus is about Latin texts and composition.
- Assume good faith. Ask for clarification before taking offense.

## Scope

This applies to all spaces: issues, discussions, pull requests, and any communication related to the project.

## Enforcement

I (the maintainer) decide what constitutes a violation and what action to take, up to and including removing comments, blocking contributors, or closing issues.
