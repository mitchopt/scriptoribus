# Conversation Skills and Topics

---

**Greeting people**
- Greet a family member in the morning
- Greet a friend you haven't seen for a long time
- Exchange morning greetings with a neighbour on the street
- Greet your patron with appropriate deference

**Saying goodbye**
- Say farewell to a family member before a journey
- Say farewell to a host after a dinner party
- Politely dismiss a client from your villa

**Asking for/giving personal details**
- Give your name, origin (*patria*), and tribe or family
- State your age and physical description
- Describe your social status and profession
- Introduce one person to another

**Ordering food or drinks**
- Order a meal at a *taberna* or *caupona*
- Ask what wine is available and choose one
- Request a specific dish and ask its price
- Complain that the food is cold or the wine watered

**Talking about the weather**
- Describe a hot summer day in the city
- Describe a storm at sea or on the road
- Comment on the cold in winter

**Saying you don't understand / Asking for clarification**
- Ask someone to speak more slowly
- Request that a word or phrase be repeated
- Ask what a word means

---

**Talking about leisure time**
- Describe a visit to the baths (*thermae*)
- Talk about attending the games (*ludi*) or gladiatorial shows
- Describe watching chariot races at the circus
- Talk about a dinner party (*convivium*)
- Describe reading or reciting poetry
- Talk about hunting or fishing
- Describe a walk in the countryside or gardens

**Talking about how often you do something**
- Describe your frequency of going to the forum
- Say how often you write letters to friends
- Talk about how regularly you exercise or study

**Expressing likes and preferences**
- Express a preference for one author over another
- Say which kind of wine, food, or entertainment you prefer
- Compare city life with country life

**Expressing dislikes and annoyances**
- Complain about the noise and crowds of the city
- Express displeasure at a bad performance or speech
- Say you dislike a certain type of person or behavior

**Describing your typical daily routine**
- Describe the morning routine: rising, dressing, greeting clients
- Talk about the hours of the day (*hora prima*, *hora sexta*, etc.)
- Describe a trip to the forum
- Talk about eating times: *ientaculum*, *prandium*, *cena*

**Talking about your work**
- Describe the work of a farmer, soldier, or merchant
- Talk about a lawyer's duties in the forum
- Describe the life of a teacher (*grammaticus* or *rhetor*)
- Talk about a craftsman's or slave's daily tasks

**Describing food**
- Describe the courses of a Roman dinner
- Talk about bread, vegetables, and staple foods of the poor
- Describe an elaborate dish at a wealthy host's table
- Talk about the production of wine or olive oil on a farm

**Describing clothing**
- Describe a toga and when it is worn
- Talk about the dress of a soldier, a priest, or a woman
- Describe mourning dress or festive clothing
- Comment on whether someone is dressed appropriately

**Describing body parts**
- Describe someone's appearance in detail
- Describe an injury or physical ailment
- Describe the appearance of a statue or painted portrait

**Talking about sports**
- Describe a chariot race and its factions
- Talk about wrestling or other athletic exercises
- Describe a gladiatorial bout
- Discuss ball games (*pila*) played at the baths

**Talking about nature**
- Describe a forest (*silva*)
- Describe a river or lake
- Talk about the sea, a harbour, or a beach
- Describe mountains and their dangers
- Talk about fields, crops, and the changing seasons
- Describe the night sky and constellations
- Talk about animals — wild, farm, or domestic

---

**Describing a building**
- Describe a Roman house (*domus*): atrium, tablinum, garden
- Describe a temple and its features
- Describe an amphitheatre, theatre, or circus
- Describe a forum and the buildings around it
- Describe a country villa or farmhouse

**Describing a place**
- Describe Rome: its hills, districts, and monuments
- Describe a provincial town or military camp
- Describe a port city or trading hub
- Describe a rural landscape: farm, vineyard, or estate
- Describe a place you have visited on a journey

**Asking for/giving directions**
- Ask how to find the forum, a temple, or an inn
- Give directions through a city using landmarks
- Ask and explain the road between two towns
- Describe a journey by sea or land

**Asking for/giving/denying permission**
- Ask a magistrate or host for permission to speak
- Grant or refuse entry to your house
- Ask permission to use someone's land or property

**Complimenting someone**
- Praise someone's oratory or writing
- Compliment a host on their dinner or hospitality
- Praise a soldier's or athlete's performance
- Flatter someone on their wisdom or learning

---

**Describing your family**
- Describe your parents, siblings, and children
- Talk about your ancestors and family history (*gens*)
- Describe family roles and duties
- Talk about marriage and household management

**Giving a description of somebody's personality**
- Describe a friend's virtues (*virtutes*)
- Describe a political enemy's vices
- Characterise a famous historical figure
- Describe the ideal Roman citizen or general

**Describing/asking about emotions**
- Express grief at a death or loss
- Describe joy at a victory or good news
- Express anger at an injustice
- Talk about fear before a battle or storm
- Describe the emotions at a wedding or festival

**Talking about health and sickness**
- Describe symptoms of illness to a doctor
- Talk about a plague or epidemic in the city
- Describe recovery from injury after battle

**Describing holidays/traditions**
- Describe a major festival: *Saturnalia*, *Lupercalia*, *Parentalia*
- Talk about sacrifices and religious rites
- Describe a triumph (*triumphus*)
- Talk about customs surrounding birth, marriage, or death

**Describing/planning a trip**
- Plan a journey from Rome to a named destination
- Describe what provisions and equipment are needed on a journey
- Talk about the hazards of travel: bandits, weather, etc.

**Making, accepting, and refusing invitations**
- Invite someone to dinner
- Accept or decline an invitation to the games
- Invite someone to accompany you on a journey
- Decline politely on grounds of duty or illness

**Describing what you find attractive/unattractive**
- Describe the ideal of beauty in a person
- Describe what makes a speech or poem pleasing or ugly
- Talk about what makes a landscape or building beautiful
- Describe virtues you admire in a person

**Describing how long something takes**
- Describe how long a journey between cities takes
- Describe the duration of a siege or campaign
- Describe the length of a festival or ceremony

---

**Talking about your childhood**
- Describe your early education: the *litterator* and *grammaticus*
- Talk about childhood games and pastimes
- Describe the town or region where you grew up
- Talk about an early memory of a festival or public event

**Describing past situations**
- Describe conditions in Rome during a war or crisis
- Talk about life before and after a major historical event
- Describe the state of a place you visited years ago
- Recount how things were done in your grandfather's time

**Describing why something in the past happened**
- Explain the causes of a war or political upheaval
- Give reasons why a general won or lost a battle
- Explain why a law was passed
- Describe why someone fell from favour

**Making comparisons**
- Compare two orators, poets, or generals
- Compare Roman customs with those of another people (Greeks, Gauls, etc.)
- Compare city life and country life
- Compare the present age with a better or worse past

**Describing the frequency of patterns**
- Describe recurring seasonal activities on a farm
- Talk about regular religious observances throughout the year
- Describe the daily patterns of a soldier on campaign

**Giving reasons**
- Argue why someone should study philosophy or rhetoric
- Give reasons for choosing one course of action over another
- Explain why a law or custom is justified
- Give reasons for undertaking a journey or military campaign

---

**Complaining**
- Complain about the behaviour of a neighbour
- Complain about corruption in public office
- Grumble about prices, taxes, or the cost of living
- Complain about a poor teacher, doctor, or tradesman

**Describing/offering a solution to a problem**
- Propose a solution to a dispute between citizens
- Suggest how to repair a damaged road or aqueduct
- Offer advice on how to manage a farm or estate better
- Propose a remedy for civic disorder

**Asking for/giving advice**
- Ask a friend for advice about a legal matter
- Give advice on how to prepare a speech
- Ask an elder for guidance on a life decision
- Advise a young man entering public life

**Warning someone about something**
- Warn a traveller about dangerous roads or brigands
- Warn a friend about an untrustworthy person
- Warn about the risks of a military or political action
- Caution someone against excess or vice

**Persuading someone**
- Persuade a friend to accompany you on a journey
- Argue for a course of action in a debate
- Try to convince a patron to support a project
- Persuade a reluctant soldier to stand firm

**Softening requests**
- Ask a favour of a social superior with appropriate deference
- Request something from a slave or freedman politely
- Soften a criticism with a compliment

---

**Expressing obligation**
- State what a soldier or magistrate is duty-bound to do
- Talk about what *pietas* demands of a Roman citizen
- Describe contractual or legal obligations

**Expressing necessity/lack of necessity**
- State that a particular sacrifice or ritual is required
- Explain that something is unnecessary or can be omitted

**Expressing possibility/impossibility**
- Say whether a sea voyage in winter is possible
- Discuss whether a military objective is achievable
- Speculate on whether a rumour could be true

**Prohibiting something**
- Issue a prohibition as a magistrate or general
- Forbid entry to a place or use of property
- State what religious law (*fas*) prohibits

**Expressing expectations**
- Say what you expect a friend, slave, or soldier to do
- State what a host is expected to provide
- Talk about what Roman custom expects of a citizen

**Expressing where something is relative to something else**
- Describe the position of buildings in a city or forum
- Give the location of a place on a route
- Describe the layout of a military camp
- Describe where something is on a body or statue

---

**Describing a hypothetical situation**
- Describe what you would do if you were consul
- Speculate on what Rome would be like without its laws
- Imagine what would have happened if a battle had gone differently
- Describe your ideal life if wealth were no object

**Reporting speech**
- Report what an ambassador or envoy said
- Relay a message from a general to his troops
- Report what a philosopher or orator argued
- Convey a rumour or piece of news heard in the forum

**Self-correcting**
- Revise a grammatical form mid-sentence
- Correct a name or date you misstated
- Revise a statement after reflection
- Acknowledge an error in an argument

**Hesitating / Filling time**
- Express that you are gathering your thoughts
- Use filler phrases while searching for the right word
- Signal that you are about to make an important point
